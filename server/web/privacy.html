Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. This policy explains what the Gaali Finder app and its server collect and why. The service is run by Aniruddha Roy ("we").

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and display name. If you sign up with a password, we store only a salted argon2id hash of it, never the password itself. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address, your name and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Sign-in sessions. Long-lived sign-in tokens, stored on our server only as hashes, and encrypted on your device.
  • Clips you upload. The audio file, its file name, and your display name as the uploader. Uploaded clips are visible to everyone who uses the app and to anyone who has the clip's link.
  • Likes and dislikes. We record which account reacted to which clip. Other users see only the totals, not who reacted.
  • Technical data. Your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to limit abuse (for example, repeated sign-in attempts). Server logs may contain IP addresses and are kept only as long as needed for security and debugging.
  • On your device. The app uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging to count usage and deliver notifications. Clips you play are cached on your device.

How we use it

To run the service: sign you in, show the shared library, enforce upload limits, and send the emails you ask for (a verification link when you sign up, and a password-reset link on request). We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Who else handles it

  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (Mumbai, India) hosts the server, database, audio files and backups.
  • Brevo sends our verification and password-reset emails, so it receives your email address and the message.
  • Google provides Google sign-in and Firebase.

How long we keep it

Your account and clips stay until you ask us to delete them. Daily backups of the database are kept for 14 days. Copies of audio files in our backups are not purged automatically, so a deleted clip may remain in a backup until we remove it.

Your choices

You can delete a clip you uploaded from inside the app. To have your account and data deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email ar.roy564@gmail.com. We will reply within a reasonable time.

Children

The app contains strong language and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
